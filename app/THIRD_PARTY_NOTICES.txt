Journal metric references
Selected numeric JIF references were provided by the user in a workbook and queried from public
AbleSci journal pages (https://www.ablesci.com/journal). Source URLs and publication-year labels
are retained. Values have not been independently verified against licensed Clarivate JCR data.
The displayed metric year 2025 follows the user's configuration; latest AbleSci pages label the
release as 2026. No full website pages, journal articles, or licensed JCR database are bundled.

CSL styles: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles (CC BY-SA 3.0; embedded rights and authors preserved in assets/csl).
citeproc-py 0.11.1 (BSD-2-Clause), lxml 6.1.3 (BSD-3-Clause). Respective license and locale files are bundled. EndNote proprietary styles and software are not bundled.

PDF import dependencies
pdfplumber 0.11.9 and pdfminer.six 20251230 provide local PDF text and font extraction.
Their licenses and those of PDFium/pypdfium2, Pillow, cryptography, cffi, pycparser and charset-normalizer
are retained under assets/third_party_licenses/pdf_import. PDF files are processed locally;
only recognized titles and DOI identifiers are used for bibliographic matching.
